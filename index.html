I am a writer studying design and learning about computation. I make poem-websites for art and work freelance in UX & technical documenation. I also really like to teach. I have lived in Hamburg nearly a decade and grew up in the US.

●artistic projects

When I write, I like to think about how I can shape text and interface as a whole. For poetry, I work simultaneously between code and audio tools to creat the sites. Reading aloud is part of the process from the beginning for me, but I also enjoy creating live versions of the text later that "perform the website" in some way, and inevitably turn out quite different.

The most recent piece The End The End The End, initially designed as a mobile website and now available for desktop. A compilation of earlier sites made up my portfolio to join the Klasse Digitale Grafik at HFBK Hamburg.

●editorial work

Building off of a background as a language and media educator, I specialize in writing informational texts and guides. Past work includes fully restructuring the help documenation for Are.na, as well as writing reports for the market research platform Statista. CV and samples are available on request.
