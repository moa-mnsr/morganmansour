I am a writer studying design, learning about computation, and living in Hamburg. I make poem-websites as art and technical documentation for work. I also really like to teach.

●artistic projects

When I write, I try to think about how I can shape text and interface as a whole and switch between code, audio, and design tools. Reading aloud is part of the writing process from the beginning for me, but I also enjoy creating live versions of the text later that "perform the website" in some way, and inevitably turn out quite different.

The most recent piece The End The End The End, initially designed as a mobile website and now available for desktop. A compilation of earlier sites made up my portfolio to join the Klasse Digitale Grafik at HFBK Hamburg.

●editorial work

Building off of a background as a language and media educator, I specialize in writing informational texts and guides. Past work includes fully restructuring the help documenation for Are.na, as well as writing reports for the market research platform Statista. CV and samples are available on request.
