Hello and welcome to my deskpage! In contrast to a homepage, this one is about work—so it starts with my real name and ends in {dot} com.

I would like to think of it though as not only com●mercial but com●munal. I think the work I want to do in the world has always been much more about com●passion and com●munication anyway.

I am a writer studying design and learning about computation. I have also taught creative code and worked in multilingual education.

I like working on texts and platforms that are meant to guide and connect people—with each other as well as all the wonderful things you can do with a phone or computer.

Some past work includes ● teaching a daylong guest workshop in a design class at Burg Halle , ● copyediting and fully restructuring the information architecture of the help documentation for Are.na, and ● writing short-form data journalism about fossil fuels and renewable energy at Statista.

I would be very glad to continue in the direction of writing technical documentation & teaching in fine arts, alongside work on literary projects. I work freelance and am always open to creative collaborations too, so please feel free to reach out if any of this speaks to you!

Teaching very young people has been really formative for me—both because it brought me a lot of joy, and also because it was a serious training in figuring out the needs the people who might not know how to communicate them yet.

This is completely understandable, not just because we were all a child once (we were though!), but because even as adults it happens all the time—especially in making and using software and interfaces.

I also like helping people build their own websites manually, in a way that offers lot of agency to shape and maintain it—whether by teaching some basic code, or setting up a friendly content management system. If you're an artist, journalist, small cultural or social organization, or anyone who needs a website with a more DIY approach, please feel free to reach out and I would be glad to design this collaboration together on a sliding scale.
