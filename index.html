I am a writer and designer working in experimental publishing and web development. I am currently studying a Master of Fine Arts at HFBK Hamburg with the Klasse Digitale Grafik.

I offer technical documentation and content design for startups and companies, and small friendly websites for organizations or individuals.

Past editorial work includes fully restructuring and revising the help documentation for Are.na, and writing data reports about energy and agricultural industries at Statista.

I also teach creative code and have worked in multilingual education. Most recently, I led a daylong guest workshop about the poetic web in a design class at BURG Halle.
