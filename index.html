Hello and welcome to my deskpage! On the smooth wooden surface, it's a site about work that starts with my name and ends in dot com.

I want to reclaim that dot to not only mean com●mercial, but com●munal. I'd like to think of websites as warm signals to future community: a soft broacast to someday friends and collaborators, or just a bench to sit down on in a forest.

So have a stroll, and feel free to say hi!
post {at} morganmansour {dot} com

I am a writer and web designer working in experimental publishing. I am currently studying a Master of Fine Arts at HFBK Hamburg in the Klasse Digitale Grafik.

In my own artistic projects, I make poem-websites that take the composition of the text and interface as a whole. With Veronika Pluzhnikova (as wwwegetables), I am also building a collective practice related to plants and networks.

I sometimes teach about net art and the poetic web, most recently in a design foundations class at BURG Halle. Right now I am planning a workshop for a few other schools (tba!), and would always love to do more of this!

I also offer technical writing and content design for startups and companies. Past work includes a full restructuring of the help documentation for Are.na.

* This site was freshly designed and coded by hand in May 2026. It was last updated in late July in Hamburg. The sky is still bright here for a long time each day, even as the summer slows. Though the color has changed, the font on the site is still Authentic Sans.
