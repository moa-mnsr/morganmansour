Morgan Mansour

Hello and welcome to one of my websites*! This is the one that starts with my name and ends in {dot} com, but I would like to think of it as not only com●mercial but com●munal.

Being helpful and making feelings of community is, after all, both the real business of living and the point of what I do for work—whether teaching creative code or making documentation and interfaces that are meant to guide and connect.

As a teacher, I have primarily worked with children and young people and really enjoyed this. I have started shifting to teaching in a university context since pursuing a master's at HFBK Hamburg and recently did a daylong guest workshop about net art in design class at Burg Halle.

As an editor, technical writing is the direction I'd like to keep going as I learn about more computation. I really enjoyed the experience restructing the help docs for Are.na and would love to do more of this. Previously, I wrote data reports at Statista about topics related to fossil fuels and renewable, and still find this super interesting on an academic level too.

I also like to help people build their own websites and digital publications in a way that offers a lot of agency to shape and maintain it over time. I'd be glad to design a collaboration like that together and think it could work well for individuals or small groups, especially for artists or cultural orgs. If you feel like this could be you, please feel free to reach out!

I speak English, German, and French, and am always happy to mix all three. I like email, and you can also find me on Are.na, Mastdon, LinkedIn, and Github.

* This site was last updated in late May 2026, and was freshly remade as a companion to an art portfolio and more experiential poetry sites. Hope to see you there too!
