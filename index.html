Hello and welcome to my deskpage! On the surface (smooth, wooden), it's a site about work that starts with my name and ends in dot com.

I want to reclaim that dot to not only mean com●mercial, but com●munal. I'd like to think of websites as warm signals to future community: a soft broadcast to someday friends and collaborators, or just a bench to sit down on in a forest.

So have a stroll, and feel free to say hi!
post {at} morganmansour {dot} com

I am a writer and designer working in experimental publishing. I am currently studying a Master of Fine Arts at HFBK Hamburg in the Klasse Digitale Grafik.

In my own artistic projects, I make poem-websites that take the text and interface as a whole. I am also learning about sound design as a further medium for poetry. With Veronika Pluzhnikova (as wwwegetables), I am building a collective practice related to plants and networks.

I also teach about the poetic web, and recently gave talks and workshops at Aalto University and BURG Halle. I would always love to do more than this! I have a background working in school, and really enjoy teaching digital arts and creative code for young learners, too.

For startups and companies, I offer technical writing and content design. Past work includes a full restructuring of the help documentation for Are.na. For individuals and organizations, I'd be glad to make you a small friendly website.
